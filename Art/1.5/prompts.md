## 伸懒腰

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: desktop fox animation sprite strip, six keyframes.
Reference image is the exact character identity: Susu, ink/navy blue fluffy chibi fox with cream muzzle/chest/paws/inner ears, amber eyes and huge navy tail with cream tip containing three linked amber markings. Match the reference polished soft illustrated rendering, proportions and face.
Create ONE transparent PNG sprite sheet: exactly 6 evenly spaced frames in a single horizontal row in a wide 3:1 canvas, all same scale, full body and entire tail, generous transparent gutters, no labels, no floor, no text or shadows outside body. Fox faces three-quarter right.
Action STRETCH: 1 calm standing quadruped, 2 reaches both forepaws forward beginning bow, 3 deep natural fox stretch forepaws extended with chest low and rump raised, 4 holds deep stretch with eyes contentedly closed and tail curved, 5 rises back up, 6 same calm standing as first. The forepaw floor contact and character center must remain aligned across all six equal cells. Pose changes must be clear anatomical movements, not sliding/scaling the whole animal. All frames fit their cell. Genuine transparent alpha background.

## 玩耍

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: six-keyframe game animation strip for the exact referenced Susu fox.
Reference is identity/style only: ink navy blue chibi fox, cream face chest paws inner ears, warm amber eyes, large blue tail with cream tip and three linked amber spots. Match exact face, palette, soft polished illustration.
Generate one wide 3:1 PNG, transparent background, SIX equally spaced separate frames in ONE row, generous empty gutters 10 percent on every side inside EACH equal-width cell, no overlap, entire ears tail paws fully visible. No text labels, no props, no scenery, no glow, no ground shadow. Clean alpha cutout, no colored fringe.
PLAY action, fox facing three-quarter right: frame1 alert playful crouch; frame2 raises one forepaw to playfully bat at the air; frame3 light playful pounce forward, both forepaws extended, happy eyes; frame4 lands in low playful bow; frame5 playful paw swipe other paw with cheerful face and wagged tail; frame6 recovers to same crouch as frame1. Natural quadruped fox anatomy. Clear changing body and leg poses; never just translate/rotate the entire sprite. Fixed camera, consistent body size, fixed notional ground plane, no horizontal locomotion.
